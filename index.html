I’m Pasindu, an Unreal Engine C++ gameplay programmer at the beginning of my career. I enjoy turning gameplay ideas into systems that can be configured, reused, and improved.

My Feature Gym project brings together reusable combat and parkour plugins. On Aeoliada, I worked with a team on AI-powered NPC dialogue and gameplay bug fixes. During my internship at RAM Studios in Sri Lanka, I contributed to multiplayer debugging and vehicle gameplay.

My background also includes web development, self-directed study in Unity and Unreal Engine, and game jams. Outside programming, I train in Kung Fu and Jiu-Jitsu. That interest in martial arts also connects with my enthusiasm for combat gameplay.

Based in Kegalle District, Sri Lanka. Interested in gameplay programming opportunities in Japan, including on-site, hybrid, and remote roles.
